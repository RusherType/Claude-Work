# 8. Implementation Plan

Six phases over 12 weeks from Monday 5 October 2026, a gate at the end of each, and a sales track alongside from day one, so the public launch in the last week of December lands with paying design partners.

## Phases and gates

1. **Phase 1 — Foundation and tariff data (weeks 1–2, 5–16 Oct).** CD-001 to CD-007, CD-010 to CD-012. Gate: sign in, create a workspace, search the HTS by meaning ("cotton men's t-shirt" finds 6109).
2. **Phase 2 — The agent (weeks 3–5, 19 Oct–6 Nov).** CD-013, CD-030 to CD-036. Gate: eval ≥ 90% at 6 digits, ≥ 75% at 10 digits, under $0.05 per SKU.
3. **Phase 3 — Catalog and review (weeks 6–7, 9–20 Nov).** CD-020 to CD-024, CD-040 to CD-042. Gate: 5 design partners synced a real store and confirmed codes.
4. **Phase 4 — Landed cost and monitor (weeks 8–9, 23 Nov–4 Dec).** CD-014, CD-015, CD-050 to CD-052, CD-060 to CD-062. Gate: 40 cost tests pass; one partner's real duty bill matches within 2%.
5. **Phase 5 — Money and polish (weeks 10–11, 7–18 Dec).** CD-070, CD-071, CD-080, CD-090, CD-091, CD-100 to CD-102; submit Shopify listing (CD-103) at start of week 10. Gate: first paid invoice.
6. **Phase 6 — Launch (week 12, 21 Dec–1 Jan).** CD-103 to CD-105, fixes. Gate: public launch on Shopify App Store, Product Hunt, LinkedIn.
